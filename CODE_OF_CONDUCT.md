# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent;
- give actionable, technically grounded feedback;
- accept constructive criticism and correct mistakes;
- protect private repository data, credentials, and vulnerability details;
- focus discussion on what is best for users and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not tolerated:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately exposing secrets, private source code, or embargoed vulnerabilities;
- sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith participation.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, security reports, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate repository abuse, use GitHub's report and block features. For a private report to the maintainers, open a private advisory at <https://github.com/Bor-Code/CodeRisk-Dashboard/security/advisories/new> and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with conflicts of interest avoided where possible.
